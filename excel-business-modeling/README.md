# Excel Business Modeling and Decision Analysis

**Portfolio title:** Business Decision Models | Goal Seek, Optimization, and Scenarios in Excel

**Portfolio description:** A collection of spreadsheet models for answering practical planning questions: the price or volume needed to break even, the product mix that maximizes profit under resource limits, and the sensitivity of results to changing assumptions.

## Project contents

- `Goal_Seek_Analysis.xlsx` — break-even and profit models using Goal Seek.
- `Product_Mix_Optimization.xlsx` — product-mix optimization and supporting problem sheets.
- `Scenario_and_Sensitivity_Analysis.xlsx` — data tables and scenario comparisons.

## Tools and methods

Microsoft Excel, Goal Seek, Solver-style linear optimization, constraints, scenario analysis, and sensitivity analysis.

## Publishing notes

These files are separate exercises bundled by topic. Add a short problem statement, assumptions, and interpreted results for each model so readers can follow the decisions and reproduce them.
